Keeping customers is cheaper than finding new ones. So I ran the numbers to find out which ones are actually leaving — and where the retention budget should go.

I analyzed 5,000 synthetic SaaS customers. The headline: 22.9% churn. But it was never evenly spread.
- Segment is everything: Startups churn at 29.3% vs 11.0% for Mid-market. A chi-square test (p = 5.07e-30) says the gap is real, not noise.
- The churned profile: shorter tenure, 2.2x support tickets, dormant logins. At 4+ tickets, churn hits 36.2%.
- LTV vs CAC (CAC assumed) tells you where to spend: Startups return 3.0x their acquisition cost; Mid-market barely breaks even at 1.0x.

The prescription: focus retention on Startups and the Basic plan, trigger outreach at 4 tickets, win back dormant logins.

SQL first, as always: extraction, aggregation, and every KPI came from SQL. Python handled the chi-square test, cohort analysis, and automation.
