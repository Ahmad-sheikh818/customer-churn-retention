22.9% of customers were churning. The fix wasn't "email everyone." It was segmentation.

I analyzed 5,000 SaaS customers:
- Startups churn at 29.3% vs 11.0% for mid-market. Chi-square test says the difference is real (p = 5.07e-30).
- Churned customers: shorter tenure, 2.2x support tickets, dormant logins. At 4+ tickets, churn hits 36.2%.
- LTV vs CAC says where to spend: startups return 3.0x their acquisition cost, mid-market barely 1.0x.

The prescription: focus retention on startups and the Basic plan, trigger outreach at 4 tickets, win back dormant logins.
